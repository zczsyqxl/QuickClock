QuickClock
==========

A fullscreen clock: press your shortcut, see the time, press ESC or Q to close.

Quick start
-----------
1. Extract the zip anywhere.
2. (Recommended) Create a shortcut to QuickClock.exe on your desktop,
   open its Properties, and set a "Shortcut key" such as Ctrl+Alt+T.
3. Press the shortcut key whenever you want the time. Press ESC or Q to dismiss.

Notes
-----
- Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. No installation, no dependencies.
- Launching it while it is already running will raise the existing window
  instead of opening a new one.
- Time is always your local timezone.
